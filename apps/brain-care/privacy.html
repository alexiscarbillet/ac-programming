Privacy policy

BrainCare does not require an account and does not send exercise history or progress to a BrainCare server. Exercise sessions and profile progress are stored locally on your device using the platform's preferences storage.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or cloud synchronization. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage may remove locally saved progress.

The app uses the Google Fonts Flutter package for typography. If font files are not available locally, the package may request them from Google Fonts; Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

BrainCare is a personal training tool, not a medical device. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment.

For questions, use the contact page.
